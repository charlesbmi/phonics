Glyph review:
The official same-version normal 400 TrueType faces were rendered at 76 px
using Pillow/FreeType and visually inspected with sample:
  a g l i j t I 1 tall little
Single-storey a: Andika, Lexend, Comic Neue.
Double-storey a: Atkinson Hyperlegible, Noto Sans, Noto Serif, Fira Mono,
JetBrains Mono.
Andika, Lexend, Noto Sans, and Comic Neue have straight lowercase l stems;
Atkinson Hyperlegible, Fira Mono, and JetBrains Mono have curved lowercase l
feet; Noto Serif has serifs. Lexend and Comic Neue have straight-ended t stems,
while Andika, Noto Sans, and Fira Mono have visibly curved t feet.
Fira Mono and JetBrains Mono are monospaced (equal advance widths checked in
TrueType hmtx for a g l i j t I 1).
These are shape descriptions, not claims about reading outcomes.
