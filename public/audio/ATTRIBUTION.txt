Four-word recording pilot

These are natural US-English recordings by Dvortygirl on Wikimedia Commons.
Timings are unreviewed PocketSphinx 5.1.1 forced-alignment candidates, not
manually verified phone boundaries. No listening review or approved sustain
regions are claimed. The selected hello pronunciation is a forced lexicon
candidate and still needs listening review.

Dad, She, and sheep originals and converted WAVs are distributed under
Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported. See CC-BY-SA-3.0.txt
and https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/.
hello was released into the public domain by its creator (PD-self).

Changes: decoded to mono 44100 Hz signed 16-bit PCM WAV, downmixed stereo
where applicable, removed metadata, retained full duration. No splicing,
speed/pitch changes, looping, or generated speech.

Creator: Dvortygirl, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/User:Dvortygirl

Exact original/processed hashes, retrieval dates, edits, source revisions,
and build tool/model identity are recorded in manifest.json and sources.json.
Original Ogg files are retained in originals/.

Rebuild from checked-in originals: uv run --locked --no-config --script scripts/prepare-audio.py
The build requires ffmpeg and uses a pinned build-time PocketSphinx package;
the browser downloads no aligner or acoustic model.
